My TikTok Got Hacked — A Step-by-Step Recovery Guide

What to do in the first 24 hours after your TikTok is compromised, how attackers lock you out, and the recovery process that actually works.

TikTok account hacks are surging. Creators with tens of thousands — sometimes millions — of followers are losing access overnight. The attacker changes the email, phone number, and password within minutes, and suddenly you are locked out of an account you spent years building.

How TikTok Accounts Get Hacked

The most common attack vectors are simpler than people think. Phishing links sent via DM that look like official TikTok notifications. SIM swapping to intercept two-factor authentication codes. Credential stuffing using passwords leaked from other platforms. And in some cases, social engineering directed at TikTok's own support channels.

Once the attacker has access, they immediately change the email address, remove the phone number, and update the password. This triple lock makes it nearly impossible to recover through TikTok's standard self-service options.

The First 24 Hours Matter

If you notice your TikTok has been compromised, act immediately:

  • Do not delete the TikTok app — you may still have an active session that can help with recovery.
  • Report the account as hacked through TikTok's "Report a Problem" form. Document everything with screenshots.
  • Secure your email accounts — if the attacker accessed your TikTok through a compromised email, change those passwords immediately and enable two-factor authentication.
  • Do not engage with the attacker — some hackers will message you from your own account offering to "sell it back." Do not pay them. This rarely results in actual account return and often leads to further scams.

Why TikTok's Standard Recovery Falls Short

TikTok's support system is built for scale, not for individual case resolution. When you report a hacked account, you enter a queue that can take weeks to process. Meanwhile, the attacker may be using your account to scam your followers, post harmful content, or simply hold it hostage.

The fundamental problem is that TikTok requires you to verify your identity to regain access — but the attacker has already changed all the verification methods linked to the account. It is a catch-22 that the standard process is not equipped to handle efficiently.

How Professional Recovery Works

Services like Cures Media resolve hacked TikTok cases through escalation channels that can verify original account ownership and override the attacker's changes. The process typically involves proving you are the original owner through account history, device history, and original registration details — information that the attacker cannot replicate.

Most hacked TikTok recoveries are completed within 24 to 72 hours. The account is returned with all content, followers, and engagement history intact.

Prevention Going Forward

After recovering your account, immediately enable TikTok's two-factor authentication using an authenticator app (not SMS). Use a unique, strong password that is not shared with any other service. And be extremely cautious of DMs containing links — even from accounts you trust, since they may be compromised too.
