How Much Is an OG Username Actually Worth?

A pricing breakdown of the OG username market across Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and more. What drives value, what to expect, and how to avoid overpaying.

The OG username market is one of the most opaque corners of the internet. Prices range from $50 for a semi-desirable five-letter handle to $50,000+ for a single real-word, two-letter Instagram username. Understanding what drives these prices is essential before you spend anything.

What Determines Username Value

Four factors drive pricing in the OG username market:

  • Character count: Fewer characters means higher value. A two-letter Instagram handle is exponentially more valuable than a five-letter one. There are only 676 possible two-letter combinations — and every single one has been registered.
  • Linguistic quality: Real English words command premiums. "@sky" is worth far more than "@xkq" even though both are three characters. Common first names, nouns, and adjectives are the most valuable.
  • Platform: Instagram usernames are generally the most valuable, followed by TikTok. YouTube handles and Facebook vanity URLs have smaller but still active markets.
  • History: Previously verified accounts, accounts with existing follower bases, or accounts with clean histories (no bans) can carry premiums.

Rough Price Ranges

These ranges are approximate and shift with market conditions, but they give a realistic picture of what to expect in 2026:

  • 2-letter Instagram handles: $5,000 — $50,000+ depending on letter combination
  • 3-letter Instagram handles: $1,000 — $10,000 depending on pronounceability
  • 4-letter real word Instagram: $500 — $5,000
  • 5-letter real word Instagram: $200 — $1,500
  • TikTok handles: Generally 30-50% lower than equivalent Instagram names
  • YouTube handles: Newer market, prices still stabilizing. Roughly 20-40% of Instagram equivalent

How to Avoid Overpaying

The biggest risk in the username market is not the price — it is paying the wrong person. Scams are rampant. Sellers who take payment and disappear. Usernames that get "reclaimed" days after transfer because the seller never actually owned the original email. Always use escrow, always verify the seller's track record, and always confirm full email swap is included in the transfer.
